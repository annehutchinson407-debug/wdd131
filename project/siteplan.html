Rooted Lexicon

Site name reason: Rooted Lexicon was chosen because it combines the idea of strong gardening foundations with a helpful learning resource. The name reflects both the growth of plants and the growth of gardening knowledge.

Purpose

Rooted Lexicon is a gardening reference site that provides definitions, plant care terms, seasonal advice, soil and compost explanations, and beginner-friendly guidance for people who want to learn and grow confidently in the garden.

Goals

The site aims to help gardeners understand common terminology, explore useful plant-care concepts, and feel more confident when choosing tools, planning beds, and caring for garden spaces.

Mission

To make gardening knowledge accessible, practical, and inspiring so that new and experienced growers can learn with confidence and enjoy healthier plants.

Target Audience

Primary audience

Beginner gardeners, home growers, and plant enthusiasts who want to learn gardening terms, techniques, and seasonal care practices for healthier plants.

Needs

  • Clear definitions for garden terms
  • Easy-to-follow care guidance
  • Mobile-friendly learning experience
  • Helpful seasonal and plant-care tips

Secondary audience

Family members, hobby gardeners, and classroom learners looking for accessible explanations of soil, compost, pruning, and plant health concepts.

Scenario Questions

  • How can a beginner gardener quickly understand what a hardiness zone or drip line means?
  • Where can a new grower find simple explanations for compost, mulching, and pruning terms?
  • What tools and seasonal tips are best for someone starting a small vegetable or flower garden at home?

Color Usage

  • Forest Green will be used for the main header, primary buttons, and key brand highlights.
  • Royal Purple will be used for accent elements, call-to-action buttons, and section dividers.
  • Soft Mint will be used as a background color in cards and content blocks to create a fresh, natural feel.
  • Deep Charcoal will be used for text, headings, and body content to ensure readability.

Typography

The site uses a clean, modern sans-serif pairing for a fresh, outdoor-inspired look that stays easy to read.
